The Practice

Shaped by place.

Crystalline Architects is an architecture, construction and facility management practice working across residential, hospitality and mixed-use projects.

Lagos, Nigeria Practice / 2026
03

Approach

One practice, from first sketch to long-term management.

Crystalline Architects provides Architectural Design and Build, Building Construction, Project Management and Facility Management.

The practice works across residential, hospitality and mixed-use projects. Each commission begins with the specific requirements of the site and the people who will use it.

Our role can extend from early design through construction and into the management of completed facilities.

Services

What we do.

Four connected areas of work, delivered individually or as part of a single commission.

01

Architectural Design and Build

Building permit and approval, building design, consultancy and interior design.

02

Building Construction

Plumbing services, electrical services, interior finishes and decoration, and material sourcing.

03

Project Management

Building setting out, concrete slab augmentation, strength of material calculation, civil engineering drawing interpretation, mechanical drawing implementation and carcass delivery.

04

Facility Management

Ongoing management of built facilities.

Process

A clear route from brief to building.
